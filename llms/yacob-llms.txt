# Yacob

> Yacob was a Dubai-based "Intelligent Nutrition" platform and 500 Global portfolio company that combined cashless school-meal payments with nutrition monitoring: pupils paid for school meals with a stored-value card while parents topped up accounts online, tracked transactions, set spend and food limits, flagged allergies, and reviewed the nutritional value of what their children ate.

The company appears dormant: yacob.com no longer resolves (its DNS zone is
deleted and the domain served a GoDaddy parked lander by 2025). This summary is
grounded in the last live archived copies of yacob.com (2019-2021 snapshots on
the Internet Archive). No public API, developer portal, SDKs, or machine-readable
specifications were ever found for this company.
